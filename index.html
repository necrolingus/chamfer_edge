Libraries & Architecture

EdgeCraft STL runs 100% locally in your browser with zero server uploads. It is powered by the following open-source libraries and custom geometry algorithms:

Three.js
v0.169.0 MIT License

Provides the WebGL 3D rendering viewport, camera OrbitControls, binary/ASCII STLLoader, and STLExporter.

Manifold-3D (WebAssembly)
v3.0.0 Apache-2.0 License

High-performance WebAssembly solid modeling kernel by Emmett Lalish. Guarantees watertight manifold 3D boolean CSG subtraction (for convex outside edges) and boolean union (for concave inside corners).

EdgeCraft Topology & Rational Bézier Engine
Built-in ES Module First-Party

Custom client-side half-edge mesh analyzer that welds raw STL triangle soup, classifies convex vs. concave dihedral creases, heals split STL face edges into continuous loops, and sweeps mitered chamfer & exact rational quadratic Bézier circular fillet volumes.
